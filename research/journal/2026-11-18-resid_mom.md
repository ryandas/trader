## What happened
Reasonable ranking skill (beats random picks) but it is mostly momentum under another name (0.87 correlation) and the
default momentum already earns about the same here (9.6% at INR 5,000). Merit at 100,000 is "no" because one grid
point (126 days) does not beat the index. Nothing here suggests residual momentum adds to what momentum already gives.
