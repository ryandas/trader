# high_52w (2026-11-19)

**Verdict: FAIL** (trial #3)

## Hypothesis
Anchoring: investors under-react to good news when a stock is near its 52-week high, so stocks closest to their high keep outperforming. Score = close / trailing 252-bar max close; higher = buy.

## Source
George and Hwang (2004), Journal of Finance 59(4) (abstract seen via search 2026-11-19). Gupta, Locke, Scrimgeour (2010, 51 countries; positive but below conventional momentum) seen cited on a broker page, not opened. Seed idea was written from US-centred memory. Published effect may have weakened.

## Checks before testing (no backtest results used)
- (a) Outside the US: no Indian portfolio ranking study found in `research/india_evidence.md`; an unattributed Indian study (Oct 2004-Aug 2023) is described secondhand as showing near-high stocks earning higher returns and Sharpe. Weak evidence.
- (b) Small-account fit: top_n 10, buffer 5; monthly or quarterly cadence. Monthly turnover is moderate, so DP charges are a real drag at INR 5,000; quarterly is cheap.

## Grid (fixed before evaluation)

## What we learned

A partly cost-related effect: charges were 2.22%/yr at 5,000 and the same rules reached 14.6% at 25,000 and 16.5% at 100,000 (beating the index, but still below the basket at 25,000, and only 4/6 years; merit at 100k failed on the grid gate because the quarterly point was weak). Correlation with default momentum is 0.72, so it largely repeats momentum. Exposure was low in 2022 and 2026 (bear exit). Per the rules, no re-tuning this session; any variant is a new trial. Backtest has survivorship bias; not evidence either way.
