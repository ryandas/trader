# str_rev (2026-11-18), FAIL (trial 5)

**Hypothesis.** Short-horizon overreaction: the worst 21-day performers bounce over the following weeks.
**Source.** Quantpedia, "Short Term Reversal Effect in Stocks" (page opened, summary only); Terblanche (2015), JSE large caps (abstract). No Indian test found (research/india_evidence.md not updated).
**Small-account fit (before testing).** 2 or 5 positions, so each is about INR 2,500 or 1,000; weekly vs monthly cadence.
**Grid (fixed before eval).** lookback 21; top 2 buffer 1 and top 5 buffer 2; rebalance W and M; bear_exit on.

## What we learned
- The reversal did not produce a positive edge in this window. Losers kept losing.
- Costs were the larger problem: 4.4% a year at 5,000, falling to 3.5% at 100,000. Weekly turnover burns the account (57% a year in 2021, 14 to 18% in later years); the best monthly point (top 5) reached only 2.5%, below the index.
- The idea is not a good fit for an account of this size. A revived version would need a much lower turnover or a different signal, and would count as a new trial.
- Published reversal effects are for US and South African large caps, often before costs. The NSE result here is the first local evidence, and it is negative.
