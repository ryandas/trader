# 2026-11-18: high_52w (trial #2) - FAIL

- **Hypothesis**: stocks near their 52-week high keep outperforming (anchoring: under-reaction to good news).
- **Source**: George & Hwang (2004), J. Finance 59(5), 2145-2176 (citation checked by search).
- **Grid** (fixed before any result): window 252 (the paper's 12 months) and 126; top 10, buffer 10, bear exit, monthly.
- **Setup**: liquid top-100, INR 5,000, real costs, walk-forward, OOS 2021-01-01 to 2026-11-17.

|---|---|

## What happened
Rank by nearness to the high changes often, so the book churns (about 20 trades a year) and each sale pays the fixed
INR 15.34 DP charge on roughly INR 500. Charges alone are 6.6% a year at INR 5,000, which is why it earns 2.2%.
At INR 100,000 the same rule earns 16.5%, above the index but only 0.2 points above the equal-weight basket, which is
survivorship-flattered. The informational 100,000 run is not deflated for the number of ideas tested and has no random
control, so this is a lead worth one more trial, not evidence.

## Follow-up (a new trial if run)
A low-turnover variant (fewer slots, wider keep-buffer, or quarterly rebalance) would address the charges. Note I did not
include turnover knobs in this grid; adding them now is a new trial and counts.
