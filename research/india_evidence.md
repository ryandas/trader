# Evidence outside the US (running notes)

Seed/queue ideas come from US-centred literature unless noted. Searches are WebSearch snippets (full text often paywalled); what was only seen cited is marked.

## 2026-11-18 (refill round)
- Momentum in India: Sehgal & Jain (2015, Int. J. Emerging Markets) report momentum profits persisting at intermediate horizons (up to ~6 months), not explained by CAPM / FF3 (abstract seen). Dhankar & Maheshwari (NSE, 1997-2013): short-term momentum plus long-term overreaction. An ISB blog suggests India's momentum is concentrated in small caps. A claimed "IIMA 2023 study, 8.2% alpha" was NOT verifiable: ignore.
- Short-term (1-month) reversal in India: mixed; reversal seems concentrated in past losers (Parthasarathy & Sendilvelu 2022, abstract only). Turnover high: poor fit for INR 5,000.
- MAX / lottery effect: no direct replication on the Indian cross-section found; indirect evidence (Gupta & Jacob 2021: higher volatility magnifies lottery preference in India; a tail-risk study found the anomaly vanishes after controlling for beta, MAX and idiosyncratic vol). Mixed evidence in other EM.
- Frog in the pan (Da, Gurun, Warachka, RFS 2014, 27(7) 2171-2218; citation confirmed on EconPapers/IDEAS): US only; no Indian test found.
- Novy-Marx (JFE 2012, 103(3) 429-453; citation confirmed): 12-7 month returns drive momentum; challenged by Goyal & Wahal (JFQA 2015) and a January-seasonality critique. No Indian test found.
- Trend factor (Han, Zhou, Zhu, JFE 2016; seen via CXO Advisory / WUSTL profile): MA signals of many lengths; US gross 1.63%/month; turnover ~66%/month (bad fit for small accounts unless slowed down).

## 2026-11-19 (refill round)
- Low volatility / BAB in India: NSE study Jan 2001-Jun 2016 reports low-risk (large-cap) portfolios outperforming; Agarwalla et al. (2014) find significant BAB returns; an NSE 2010-2020 study finds the volatility anomaly stronger at medium/long horizons; BAB premium small vs size and value; robustness to removing small/illiquid stocks mixed (abstracts only).
- 52-week high (George and Hwang 2004, JF): no Indian portfolio test found; only an IIMA options-market anchoring paper and an unattributed 20-market study (profits in 18 of 20). A 2022 Indian study finds daily extremes are not used as anchors (different object).
- 52-week high (second search 2026-11-19): a broker page describes an unattributed Indian study (Oct 2004-Aug 2023) with near-high stocks earning higher returns and Sharpe; Gupta, Locke, Scrimgeour (2010, 51 countries) found positive 52w-high profits, below conventional momentum (seen cited, not opened). Broker pages are low-quality sources.
- Industry/sector momentum in India: Garg and Varshney (2015, Global Business Review 16(3) 494-510) report robust sector momentum 2000-2013; a CFA Institute digest (2015) on 50 NSE stocks, 2001-2012, finds positive excess returns from sector momentum (summaries only). No Indian residual-momentum test found. Original: Moskowitz and Grinblatt (1999, US).
- Regime-switch (SSRN 7044244): abstract only; no independent replication seen.

## 2026-11-19 (second refill round)
- Residual momentum in emerging markets: only an Erasmus master thesis (10 largest EMs, higher Sharpe than conventional momentum) seen via search; too weak a source to queue. Sehgal and Jain (2011): part of Indian stock momentum is captured by a sector factor (snippet).
- NSE Nifty500 Momentum 50 (launched 2024-06-04): normalized momentum score from 6- and 12-month volatility-adjusted returns, semi-annual rebalance (snippets from niftyindices pages; not opened in full).
- Sector momentum and equal-weight winners (SSRN 4000418) were tested this day; see journal.

## 2026-11-19 (third round, nse_norm_mom)
- Nifty500 Momentum 50: 50/50 average of 6- and 12-month volatility-adjusted momentum z-scores, bottom 10% by turnover ratio excluded, score x free-float cap weighting with 5% cap, semi-annual rebalance (search summary of niftyindices/Bajaj AMC/Business Standard pages; formula details from one secondary source; official PDF not opened). No independent performance test found.
- Quality factor India: SSRN 4284686 (abstract seen): QMJ four-factor alpha 0.92%/month, long-only 0.69%/month. Low-risk anomaly on Nifty 500, Mar 2003-Jun 2022 (Quarterly Review of Economics and Finance, Dec 2025; seen in search summary). Indian 52-week-high study Oct 2004-Aug 2023 on SSRN (alpha more stable than momentum; abstract snippet; already tested as high_52w).
## 2026-11-19 (fourth round, queue refill search)
- Quantpedia summaries (seen, not opened in full): Atilgan et al. across 27 EMs find medium/short-term momentum the most robust cross-sectional effect; Alpha Architect summary: short-term momentum persists in liquid stocks, reversal sits in illiquid names (near-duplicates of tested momentum ideas).

No new queueable price-only idea (Amihud needs volume, not in the prep).
